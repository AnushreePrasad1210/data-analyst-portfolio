## Data Sources
- Our World in Data CO2 Dataset: github.com/owid/co2-data
- Our World in Data Energy Dataset: github.com/owid/energy-data

## Key Findings
- Global average renewable energy share (2023): 16.93%
- Global CO2 emissions (2023): 36.96K million tonnes
- Iceland leads globally at ~78% renewable share (geothermal + hydro)
- Turkmenistan and Trinidad & Tobago sit at essentially 0%
- CO2 emissions show persistent upward trend despite 
  two temporary dips (2008 financial crisis, 2020 COVID-19)
  — both fully recovered within 1-2 years
